FUN FACTS ABOUT LPU EVENTS

  • LPU's major festivals bring together students with interests ranging from music and dance to technology, sports and literary activities.

  • YouthVibe 2026, LPU's grand global fest, featured more than 30 educational institutions and over 20 competitions and events.

Hey! Freshers , an exciting news for you

We bring you a day filled with lots of fun and learning. Visit the auditorium to witness the grand fest ever conducted.

The LPU Freshers Induction Fest (Freshmen Induction & Freshers Fiesta) is a vibrant multi-day celebration held at the Baldev Raj Mittal Unipolis and Shanti Devi Mittal Auditorium to welcome incoming students. The event kicks off with the Wild Welcome Concert & DJ Night, a high-octane neon music festival featuring live electronic dance music and international artists; it requires a paid entry ticket and helps students learn cultural adaptation, interpersonal communication, and large-scale peer networking.

For students looking to showcase their individual skills, the Freshers Talent Hunt offers a completely free, open stage for music, dance, theatre, and comedy, teaching participants stage confidence, public speaking, and creative expression. Intellectual development is fostered through the Inquisitive Minds Contest, a free online and classroom-based challenge consisting of essays and logical puzzles that teaches critical thinking, structured reasoning, and data privacy awareness.


The celebration continues with student-led spectacles like the Graveyard Carnival & Fashion Show , a themed mega-festival featuring horror attractions and walking runways; this requires a paid ticket and introduces freshers to crowd management dynamics, community building, and event coordination. On the academic and professional front, the mandatory LPU Engage & Career Orientation is a free session featuring senior university leaders and global alumni that teaches corporate preparedness, placement strategies, and navigation of the University Management System (UMS).This years' freshers day is a free gift

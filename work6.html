Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a leading private university located in Phagwara, Punjab, India. Established in 2005, LPU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various fields, including engineering, management, computer science, sciences, law, and humanities.

The university is known for its large and modern campus, diverse student community, advanced laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, and technology-enabled learning environment. LPU also focuses on practical education, innovation, research, entrepreneurship, and industry exposure to help students develop both academic and professional skills.

With students coming from different parts of India and several other countries, LPU provides a multicultural environment where students can learn, participate in extracurricular activities, and develop their communication and leadership skills.

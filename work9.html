About Us

Skeptical Space is an organization dedicated to giving qualified and passionate students the opportunity to experience space exploration. We believe that curiosity, scientific thinking, and the courage to question the unknown can inspire the next generation of explorers. Through rigorous selection, training, and space missions, Skeptical Space aims to take students beyond Earth and closer to the mysteries of the universe.

Who We Are

Skeptical Space is a student-focused space exploration organization built around one simple idea: the future of space exploration belongs to curious minds. We identify talented students with strong scientific interests and provide them with opportunities to learn, train, experiment, and participate in space missions.

From classrooms to cosmos

The Skeptical Space Student Program is designed for students who demonstrate exceptional curiosity, academic ability, problem-solving skills, and a passion for science and technology. Selected students undergo specialized training before being considered for space missions.


Selection Process


01 — Apply

🎈Students submit their application and academic profile.

02 — Qualify

🎈Candidates are evaluated through scientific, technical, and problem-solving assessments.

03 — Train

🎈Selected candidates undergo physical, technical, and mission-oriented training.

04 — Prepare

🎈Students work with experts and participate in simulations and experiments.

05 — Explore 🚀

🎈Qualified candidates participate in an approved space mission.
